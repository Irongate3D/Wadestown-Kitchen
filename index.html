Our Story

A kitchen built on community

Since 2005, Wadestown Kitchen has been part of the fabric of this neighbourhood. What started as a full café has evolved into something more personal — run by Curtis and Kathy, it's a place where you know the people behind the counter and the food is genuinely made from scratch.

Everything in the cabinet is baked in-house fresh each day. The frozen meals are made with quality ingredients and frozen immediately to lock in the flavour. And if you need something unexpected, there's even a 3D printing table to browse while you wait for your coffee.

Small, warm, and genuinely local — that's Wadestown Kitchen.
